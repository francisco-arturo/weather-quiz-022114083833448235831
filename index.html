      About this prototype

      Practice, not predictions.

      Weatherwise is a short educational quiz for students in Switzerland. It uses three common weather emergencies to practise safer decisions. It does not issue warnings and cannot tell you what is happening outside right now.

      For current information

      Check MeteoSwiss for weather warnings and Alertswiss for official alerts and instructions from the authorities.

      If someone is in danger

      Call 112. The fire service is 118 and an ambulance is 144. Follow the instructions of the emergency services.
